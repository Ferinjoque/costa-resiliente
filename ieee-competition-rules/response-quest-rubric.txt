IEEE Response Quest Evaluation Rubric
All submissions will be evaluated using the five criteria below. Each criterion is scored on a 1–5 scale, where 5
represents exceptional performance. All Categories are weighted equally. These criteria apply to both the
Concept Phase and the Product Phase, with definitions appropriate to each stage.
